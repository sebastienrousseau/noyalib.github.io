## Licence

noyalib and its five companion crates are released under the Apache License,
Version 2.0 or the MIT License, at your option. Both texts ship in every
repository and in every crate published to crates.io. You may use, modify and
redistribute the software under either licence with no account, key or fee.

Contributions are accepted under the same terms. The
[contributing guide](https://github.com/sebastienrousseau/noyalib/blob/main/CONTRIBUTING.md)
and the [governance document](https://github.com/sebastienrousseau/noyalib/blob/main/GOVERNANCE.md)
say how decisions are made and who makes them.

## Privacy

This website is static files served by GitHub Pages. It sets no cookies, runs
no analytics and loads nothing from a third-party host. There is no contact
form and no newsletter list, so no address is ever collected.

The playground runs the parser inside your browser tab. Nothing you paste is
sent anywhere, and there is no noyalib-operated service that could receive it.

GitHub, which hosts the site and the source, has its own
[privacy statement](https://docs.github.com/en/site-policy/privacy-policies/github-general-privacy-statement).

## Trade marks

YAML is a data serialisation language whose specification is published by the
YAML project. Rust is a trade mark of the Rust Foundation. serde and
serde_yaml are the names of independent projects. noyalib is an independent
open-source project and is not affiliated with any of them.

## Security reports

Please report a security issue privately. The
[security policy](https://github.com/sebastienrousseau/noyalib/blob/main/SECURITY.md)
